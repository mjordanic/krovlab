Give a building footprint in metres and a pitch in degrees. Get back a hipped roof, or a named failure. Source on GitHub.

Units: metres and degrees.

Choose a named footprint from the list, or draw cells on the plan. Click vertices of the active cell, close the ring, then add another cell the same way. Tick Edit vertices to type millimetres when a click is too coarse.

Each row is one wall. Enter pitch as degrees, rise:run (4:12), or a percentage. Apply to all fills every row. Gable is a vertical wall (pitch 90).
